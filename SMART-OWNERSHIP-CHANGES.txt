WSWSS Smart Product Ownership — 2026-08-12

Root-cause change
- Removed fixed "top 28%" section ownership thresholds.
- Product ownership is now decided once per animation frame from the full viewport state.

Ownership score
- 62%: visible pixels as share of viewport
- 30%: visible share of the section
- 8%: focus affinity (tiebreaker only)
- Fully visible section receives a small clarity bonus.

Stability rules
- Hysteresis prevents flip-flopping at section boundaries.
- A tiny section sliver cannot steal ownership.
- If the current owner is almost gone, hysteresis releases immediately.
- A short semantic edge lock (8% of viewport, capped at 72px) keeps Hero ownership at the page start and final-section ownership at the page end.
- Outside that small edge state, ownership immediately returns to visibility scoring.
- Near top/bottom, the focus point smoothly shifts toward the document edge and remains only a tiebreaker.

- Zero-opacity atomic Standard <-> Section 3 image swap.

- Arc motion.
- 82% overshoot / shock landing.
- Transform-based traveler motion.
