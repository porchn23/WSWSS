01

Technologies Used by This Website

In this policy, “cookies” also includes browser-storage technologies that perform similar functions, such as localStorage WSWSS currently uses browser storage to remember certain preferences, such as language and your consent choices.

02

Necessary

This category is used to provide website functions you request and remember settings necessary for your experience, such as language and consent status. It is always active because disabling it may prevent parts of the website from working as selected.

03

Analytics

Analytics can be used to understand website usage, such as visit counts, pages of interest, and website performance. WSWSS does not currently use Analytics tools on this website. If such tools are added later, they will not run before you provide consent where consent is required.

04

Marketing

Marketing technologies can be used to measure advertising or tailor it to users. WSWSS does not currently use Marketing tracking or advertising pixels on this website. If added later, they will be tied to the consent status you choose.

05

How You Control Your Choices

When you first visit the website, you can choose “Accept all”, “Necessary only”, or “Settings”. You can change your choice later using the

Deleting site data or local storage in your browser settings may cause the website to forget your previous choices and display the consent banner again.

06

Consent Storage

The WSWSS consent system stores your selected status in your browser under the key wswss-consent-v1 It records only whether Necessary, Analytics, and Marketing are allowed, together with the time your settings were last updated. This system does not create user-tracking cookies by itself.

07

External Services

Following a link to LINE, TikTok Shop, or another external website takes you outside WSWSS. Those services may use their own cookies or technologies under their own policies. Your WSWSS settings do not control cookies used after you leave for an external service.

08

Changes to This Policy

If the website adds Analytics, Marketing, or another service that affects cookies or browser storage, we will update this page and the consent controls to reflect actual use.
