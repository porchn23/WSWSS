01

Data Controller

The WSWSS website is operated by PAEDSAEN PRODUCTION as the data controller for personal data we receive and process for the purposes described in this policy.

For questions about personal data or to exercise your rights, contact m8saen@gmail.com

02

Information We May Collect

The main website does not contain a registration form or a direct personal-data entry form. However, when you contact us or place an order through LINE, TikTok Shop, or another relevant channel, we may receive information necessary to provide the service, such as:

  • Your name, recipient name, delivery address, phone number, or other contact details you provide.
  • Order details, products ordered, purchase channel, and delivery information.
  • Payment evidence or information necessary to verify a transaction, without requesting more data than necessary.
  • Messages, photos, or other evidence you send when making an inquiry, reporting a problem, or requesting a return.
  • Technical information that hosting or internet service providers may process in the ordinary course of providing and securing the service.
03

Purposes of Processing

We use information only as necessary to respond to your requests and provide services, including confirming orders, arranging delivery, communicating about orders, verifying payments, handling complaints, processing exchanges or returns, preventing abnormal or fraudulent use, and complying with applicable legal obligations.

If we later use data for another purpose that requires consent, we will request consent in advance where appropriate.

04

Legal Bases for Processing

Processing may rely on the legal basis appropriate to the activity, such as taking steps at your request before entering into a contract or performing a contract, complying with legal obligations, pursuing legitimate interests that do not disproportionately affect your rights, or obtaining consent where required by law.

05

Sharing with Necessary Service Providers

We may share only the information necessary with service providers or platforms involved in your transaction, such as delivery providers, payment systems used by your chosen channel, or the platform on which the order was placed. Those providers may have their own privacy policies.

06

Data Retention

We retain data only for as long as necessary for the purpose for which it was collected, service delivery, dispute handling, transaction verification, and compliance with legal obligations. When the information is no longer needed and there is no lawful reason to retain it, we will delete, destroy, or anonymize it as appropriate.

07

Security

We use administrative and technical measures appropriate to the nature of the data and the risks involved to help protect against loss, unauthorized access, use, alteration, or disclosure. However, no method of transmitting information over the internet can guarantee complete security.

08

Your Data Protection Rights

Subject to applicable legal conditions and exceptions, you may have rights including:

  • Right of access and to request a copy of your personal data.
  • Right to rectification of inaccurate or outdated information.
  • Right to erasure or deletion when there is no lawful reason to continue retaining the data.
  • The right to request restriction of processing in certain circumstances.
  • Right to object to processing where applicable law provides that right.
  • The right to withdraw consent for activities based on consent, without affecting processing carried out before withdrawal.
  • The right to data portability where the legal requirements are met.

You can submit a request to m8saen@gmail.com We may request additional information to verify your identity and the scope of your request before taking action.

09

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy when our services, operations, or applicable requirements change. The latest revision date will be shown on this page. If a change is material, we will use an appropriate method to notify you based on the nature of the change.
